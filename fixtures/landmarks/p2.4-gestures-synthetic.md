# P2.4 synthetic gesture fixture

Hand-scripted feature signals, not a recording of a person. The zero landmark array is intentional: this replay evaluates blendshape timing; EAR geometry has separate pure unit tests. Matrix is identity, timestamps are 50 ms apart, inference is simulated at 1 ms. No pixels or audio exist.

The accompanying labels use the P2.1 session format. Five bilateral closures last 400, 600, 800, 1000 and 1500 ms; five right winks last 250, 350, 500, 800 and 1000 ms. Each has 500 ms of open eyes before closure and 1000 ms afterward. Natural blinking, silent reading and simulated talking each last ten seconds. Natural/reading closures are 100/150 ms. Talking combines 600 ms closures with alternating jaw scores 0.65/0.9.

`pnpm test tools/replay/src/gesture-fixtures.test.ts --reporter=verbose --silent=false` prints the requested table. Expected: 5/5 deliberate blinks, 5/5 right winks, no other clicks. Rates extrapolated from ten-second synthetic segments are not real human false-click measurements.
